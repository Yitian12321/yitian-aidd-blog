I am **Yitian Xiao**, an AIDD (AI-driven drug discovery) intern at XtalPi and a ZJU–CMU 25Fall student. I write about AI for protein design: new models, new papers, and the slowly shifting boundary of what can be designed rather than discovered.

I publish in Chinese on WeChat under the account **AIDD小白随想录**. This site is the English version of those posts. Figures are carried over from the originals, technical content is kept unchanged, and each post links back to the Chinese source so you can compare wording if something reads oddly.

## What I write about

- **Binder design**: hallucination-style methods, diffusion-based generators, and the loss functions that make them work.
- **Structure prediction**: what AlphaFold2 and AlphaFold3 make possible downstream, and where their confidence metrics mislead.
- **Paper notes**: reading notes on new work in AI4Protein, including the parts that do not work yet.

## Corrections

These are learning notes, not expert reviews. If you find an error, a mistranslation, or a claim that overstates the evidence, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Yitian12321/yitian-aidd-blog/issues) on the repository. I would rather be corrected than be quoted.

## How this site is built

Plain Markdown, a small Python generator, and GitHub Pages. No trackers, no analytics, no third-party scripts. The source lives at [github.com/Yitian12321/yitian-aidd-blog](https://github.com/Yitian12321/yitian-aidd-blog).
