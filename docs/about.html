Yitian Xiao

Hallucination-based AI4Protein design · protein and antibody engineering · computational biology

yitianxi@andrew.cmu.edu · GitHub · WeChat: AIDD小白随想录

I work on hallucination-based AI4Protein design: using structure prediction models as differentiable scoring functions to search sequence space for new antibodies and binders, and following those designs into the wet lab. Most recently I built XDCRCraft for de novo antibody design at XtalPi and ComplexDiff for protein-glue design at Shanghai AI Lab. I am starting an M.S. in Computational Biology at Carnegie Mellon, after a B.S. in Bioinformatics at Zhejiang University.

Experience

  1. Apr 2025 – Apr 2026
    XtalPi AI Drug Discovery Algorithm Development Intern, AI4Protein IDD Shanghai
    • Built XDCRCraft, a BindCraft-based de novo antibody hallucination model that integrates an AbLang2 antibody-naturalness prior; roughly doubled the average sequence pass rate.
    • Deployed XDCRCraft across commercial antibody programs for Eli Lilly, AbbVie and Johnson & Johnson, delivering de novo sequences against five targets with successful expression and binding.
    • Led internal antibody design programs end to end, from computational design and screening through sequencing submission for wet-lab validation; advanced four candidates to mouse in vivo studies.
    • Reproduced and optimized SAbDab-based TAP/TNP developability metrics on internal data and integrated the tool into the CADD sequence triage workflow.
  2. Apr 2026 – Aug 2026
    Shanghai AI Lab Research Intern, Artificial Intelligence Research Department Shanghai
    • Developed ComplexDiff, a dual-target protein-glue design framework with target-relative SE(3) geometric conditioning and MaSIF-derived interface residues represented as fixed point clouds.
    • Designed the framework for three scenarios: small-molecule glues, redesign of existing complexes, and construction of ternary complexes from pairs of binary complexes.
